Marketing Expo 2026

Twenty-four businesses share one gymnasium for a single day.

Date
Tuesday, November 24, 2026
Time
9:00 AM to 1:30 PM
Venue
U Gymnasium, Saint John's Junior College
Admission
Free

Programme

  1. 9:00 AM

    Doors open

    Enter through the main entrance or the side entrance.

  2. 9:30 AM

    Opening remarks

    Welcome from the MC podium, in front of the stage.

  3. 10:00 AM

    Booths open

    Walk the floor and visit all 24 booths.

  4. 11:30 AM

    Judging

    Judges tour the booths. Visitors can keep browsing.

  5. 12:45 PM

    Awards

    Prizes for best overall group and most viable product or service.

  6. 1:30 PM

    Close

    The Expo ends.
